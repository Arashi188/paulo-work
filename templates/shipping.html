Shipping Process

  1. Order Confirmation: You'll receive an order confirmation via WhatsApp immediately after placing your order.
  2. Processing: Orders are processed within 24 hours of confirmation.
  3. Dispatch: You'll receive a notification with tracking information when your order is dispatched.
  4. Delivery: Our delivery partner will contact you to arrange delivery.

Free Shipping

Enjoy free shipping on orders above ₦50,000! This offer applies to standard delivery within Lagos and major cities in Nigeria.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive:

  • A tracking number via WhatsApp
  • Link to track your package online
  • Estimated delivery date
  • Contact information of the delivery agent
