Returns & Refunds Policy

7-Day Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you're not happy with your order, you can return it within 7 days of delivery.

Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, items must meet the following criteria:

  • Return request initiated within 7 days of delivery
  • Item is unused and in original condition
  • Original packaging intact
  • All tags and labels still attached
  • Proof of purchase available

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Perishable goods
  • Personal care items (due to hygiene reasons)
  • Customized or personalized products
  • Gift cards
  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Items damaged due to misuse

Return Process

  1. Contact Us: WhatsApp us at +234 708 802 8747 within 7 days of delivery.
  2. Provide Details: Share your order number, item(s) to return, and reason for return.
  3. Get Approval: We'll review your request and provide a Return Authorization (RA) number.
  4. Pack Item: Securely pack the item with original packaging and include your RA number.
  5. Ship Back: Send the package to our returns address (you'll receive this after approval).

Refund Options

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, you can choose:

Option Processing Time Notes
Original Payment Method 5-7 business days Refund to bank account/card
Store Credit Immediate Can be used for future purchases
Exchange 3-5 business days Subject to availability

Shipping Costs for Returns

  • Defective/Wrong Item: We cover return shipping costs.
  • Changed Mind: Customer covers return shipping costs.
  • Exchange: Customer covers return shipping; we cover shipping of new item.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item:

  1. Take photos of the damage/defect within 24 hours of delivery
  2. WhatsApp the photos to us at +234 708 802 8747
  3. Include your order number in the message
  4. We'll arrange for a replacement or refund immediately

Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong item:

  1. Contact us within 24 hours of delivery
  2. We'll arrange for pickup of the wrong item
  3. The correct item will be dispatched immediately
  4. No shipping charges will apply

Cancellations

You can cancel your order if it hasn't been shipped yet:

  • WhatsApp us immediately with your order number
  • Full refund will be processed within 3-5 business days
  • Once shipped, orders cannot be cancelled (must follow return process)
Important: Items returned without prior authorization will not be accepted and may be returned to sender at their expense.
